# AI GPU Recovery Agent

**Money Talks 2 · Money Operations · Built for the CFO**

One failed GPU. Three money flows. One carbon record. One agent that executes and reconciles the entire job.

## The problem

A data center GPU fails. The warranty claim, the replacement sale, and the dead unit's resale usually live in three different systems, and nobody reconciles them. The CFO can't see what the failure really cost, or what it saved.

## How it works

**1. Call and book.** An ElevenLabs voice agent takes the failure report, finds the purchase and active protection plan, checks replacement stock, and books the service slot.

**2. Diagnose and confirm.** The technician uploads diagnostics. The agent verifies the covered failure against the policy and issues a **$12,000 warranty credit**. The unit's weight is recorded in kg — the quantity the carbon math runs on.

**3. Upgrade and collect.** The customer is offered a like-for-like $18,000 replacement or a $22,000 newer model that draws less power per workload. After the credit and $500 installation, the agent invoices **$10,500** and updates inventory.

**4. Refurbish and resell.** The agent compares approved buyers' bids for the failed unit, accepts the best net offer (**$4,500** in the demo), and books collection. The treatment route (refurbished for reuse, parts harvested, or materials recycled) is recorded with buyer evidence.

**5. Reconcile and close.** All three money flows are matched as received, and the carbon is reconciled the Soventify way: activity family named, EPA factors, units checked before multiplying, avoided CO2e shown as baseline minus refurbishment route with both sides listed, every figure carrying its source.

## Demo target

**$27,000** in matched receipts: $10,500 customer + $12,000 warranty + $4,500 resale. Plus a live carbon and energy ledger: avoided CO2e in kg and avoided energy cost from the more efficient replacement, each with its source attached. Missing evidence means pending, not verified.

## Carbon methodology

Carbon methodology from Soventify. Every carbon number follows five rules: name the activity family, use the jurisdiction's authority (US: EPA), multiply only when units match, show avoided emissions as baseline minus treatment route, and pin a source to every figure. No source, no number.
