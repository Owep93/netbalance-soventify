# Revenue Growth Agent

**AI that protects margins, captures missed value, and reconciles every transaction for the CFO.**

## Market Problem

CFOs need to protect profitability and cash flow, but customer incidents create financial decisions across service, sales, and finance. Credits can go unused, asset value can be lost, and payments can remain unmatched. Finance is left chasing teams to understand the actual margin, cash collected, and outstanding balances.

## Use Case

The CFO of a datacenter company asks:

**How do we minimize the financial impact of this incident, capture available value, and ensure everything is settled?**

Netbalance coordinates warranty credits, replacement funding, and GPU resale, then reconciles the financial outcome.

## How It Works

**1.** A customer reports a cooling leak, and the agent coordinates assistance.
**2.** The inspection identifies a **$12,000 warranty credit** for the failed unit under its protection plan.
**3.** PRISM requests human approval before transferring the credit between accounts.
**4.** The customer compares replacement options, a like-for-like HX80 at $18,000 or the HX90 upgrade at $22,000, using the warranty credit.
**5.** Netbalance selects the GPU resale offer with the highest net proceeds after shipping.
**6.** It reconciles the transactions and shows the CFO the contribution, cash received, and outstanding balance.

## Tools

**ElevenLabs** handles the customer conversation. **Nicrron** supports inspection and replacement recommendations. **PRISM** controls credit transfers through human approval. **Agora** supports competing GPU resale offers. **Netbalance** coordinates transactions, reconciliation, and CFO reporting.

## Business Value

Protect margins, improve cash visibility, and reduce the manual work required to financially close customer incidents.

## Demo KPIs

- **$12,000** — Warranty credit applied
- **$22,500** — Replacement sale ($22,000 HX90 + $500 installation)
- **$4,500** — Net resale proceeds (best of 3 Agora bids, net of fee)
- **$1,008/mo** — Avoided energy cost (HX90 vs HX80 on the metered baseline)
- **3** — Money flows reconciled (replacement, warranty, resale)
- **$0** — Outstanding

## Verified Outcome

The CFO sees **$27,000 in total matched receipts** across 3 reconciled flows, with supporting records for every transaction. The warranty credit is applied on the replacement invoice, not counted twice.

All transactions, integrations, and financial outcomes are simulated for the demo.
